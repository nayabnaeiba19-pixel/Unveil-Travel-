# ✦ Unveil Travel

### Explore Beyond Ordinary

**Unveil Travel** is a modern travel agency website designed to inspire travelers to discover extraordinary, mysterious, and hard-to-reach destinations around the world.

This project was developed as a **team project by four members**, combining modern web design, responsive layouts, interactive components, and JavaScript functionality to create an immersive travel experience.

---

## 🌍 About The Project

Unveil Travel is a travel agency website focused on destinations that are far from ordinary.

The website introduces visitors to:

- 🌋 Unusual and mysterious destinations
- 🏔️ Remote and hard-to-reach places
- 🌊 Extraordinary natural landscapes
- ✈️ Unique travel experiences
- 🧭 Different types of adventurous journeys

The main goal of the project is to create a visually immersive experience that makes users feel like they are beginning a journey before they even book their trip.

## 🧩 Website Sections

The project is divided into **4 main sections**, each focusing on a different part of the travel experience.

### 01. Destinations

A collection of extraordinary destinations around the world.

Users can explore places that are unusual, remote, mysterious, or difficult to reach.

---

### 02. Trip Types

Different travel experiences are presented through interactive cards.

Examples include:

- 🚂 Railway Journeys
- 🚢 Cruise Trips
- 🚗 Road Trips
- 🏔️ Mountain Adventures
- 🏝️ Beach Escapes

---

### 03. Get Inspired

A visual section designed to inspire users through unique travel stories, breathtaking locations, and unforgettable experiences.

The purpose of this section is to encourage visitors to discover destinations beyond traditional tourist locations.

---

### 04. Special Offers

A dedicated section for travel packages, special deals, and exclusive experiences.

Users can explore available offers and discover new ways to plan their next adventure.
